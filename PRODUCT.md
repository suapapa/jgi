# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Busy retail investors scanning market sentiment on mobile during commute or trading hours. They need to quickly digest overall market sentiment, hot stocks, and main topics on-the-go.

## Product Purpose
Summarize K-stock gallery post metadata and top-ranked post bodies using an LLM to generate daily sentiment reports, helping retail investors understand market dynamics without reading thousands of raw posts.

## Brand Personality
Analytical, sharp, and terminal-adjacent. It maintains a clean, professional dark mode native to financial tools, delivering raw yet structured sentiment data.

## Anti-references
SaaS cliché layouts with excessive card grids, giant neon-gradient text headers, warm-beige cream themes, and meaningless side-stripe borders.

## Design Principles
1. **Utility First**: Display data density with clear hierarchical typographic contrast. No unnecessary whitespace or oversized graphics.
2. **Contextual Color**: Use green (bull), red (bear), and yellow (mixed) indicator colors intentionally to code market sentiment at a glance.
3. **Optimized for Scannability**: Use clean tables, bold takeaways, and high text-to-bg contrast so users can read the report within 2 minutes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict WCAG AA contrast ratio compliance (especially for green/red/yellow text on dark background).
- Fully responsive for mobile devices.
- Support for reduced-motion settings (preventing scroll-animations for dot backgrounds).
